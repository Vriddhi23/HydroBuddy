# HydroBuddy 1.9.0 — Desktop Beta 💧

A little company. A gentle reminder to drink water.

HydroBuddy lives on your desktop, peeks around the edge, walks over to remind you to hydrate, and reacts when you answer. It stays out of the way while you work.

## Make your buddy yours

- Choose a female or male character.
- Customize T-shirt and jeans colors with swatches or a color picker.
- Preview the animation before saving.
- Edit your buddy's name and reminder message.

## Gentle reminders, on your terms

- Choose 30, 45, 60 or 90-minute reminders.
- Respond with **Yes** or **Not yet**, or dismiss with the cross.
- Test a reminder whenever you like.
- Pause, resume, open Settings or quit from the system tray / Mac menu bar.
- Enable startup at login if you want HydroBuddy to join you each day.

Everything runs locally. No accounts, analytics, cloud storage or AI service is required. Your preferences stay on your computer. Face uploads and face replacement are not included.

## Choose your download

| Computer | Download |
|---|---|
| Windows x64 | `HydroBuddy-Setup.exe` |
| Apple Silicon Mac, macOS 13+ | `HydroBuddy-1.9.0-mac-arm64.dmg` |
| Intel Mac, macOS 13+ | `HydroBuddy-1.9.0-mac-x64.dmg` |

On Windows, run the installer. On Mac, open the DMG, drag HydroBuddy to Applications, and launch it there. No developer tools are needed. Check **About This Mac** if you're unsure which Mac download to choose.

## Beta notes

- Mac support is experimental and needs feedback across real devices.
- The Windows installer is unsigned. Mac builds have a local test signature and are not Apple-notarized, so operating-system security warnings or blocks are possible.
- Mac startup at login may not work reliably until Developer ID signing and notarization are completed.
- Automatic app updates are not included; download and install a newer release when available.
- Display placement uses the primary screen. Fullscreen, multiple-display and sleep/resume behavior need broader testing.

Found a problem? [Open an issue](https://github.com/Vriddhi23/HydroBuddy/issues) with your operating-system version, Mac chip if applicable, screen scaling, the steps you followed, and a screenshot or short recording when useful.
