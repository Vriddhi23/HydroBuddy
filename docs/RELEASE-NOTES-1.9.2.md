# HydroBuddy 1.9.2 — Desktop Beta

This update fixes T-shirt colors leaving white patches on the sleeves and sides of your companion, especially when waving or celebrating. Both characters now use pose-specific bottle protection so clothing colors cover the shirt while preserving the bottle artwork.

Includes custom reminder intervals from 1 to 1,440 minutes, male and female companions, shirt and jeans colors, a system tray menu, and optional launch at login. Everything runs locally without accounts or a backend.

## Downloads

- **Windows:** `HydroBuddy-Setup.exe`
- **Apple silicon Mac:** `HydroBuddy-1.9.2-mac-arm64.dmg`
- **Intel Mac:** `HydroBuddy-1.9.2-mac-x64.dmg`

Only attach and announce installers that have been built and tested. Mac support remains experimental until verified on physical Macs. Windows builds are unsigned; Mac beta builds are ad-hoc signed and not notarized, so operating-system security warnings may appear.

Existing preferences are preserved when updating. Quit HydroBuddy from its tray menu before installing the update.
