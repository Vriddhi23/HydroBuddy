# HydroBuddy 1.9.2 — Desktop Beta 💧

A little company while you work, and a reminder to take a sip.

HydroBuddy peeks around the edge of your screen, walks over to remind you to drink water, and reacts when you answer. It runs as a desktop app and keeps working when Settings is closed.

## What's included

- Female and male companions with customizable T-shirt and jeans colors.
- A preview before saving, plus your own buddy name and reminder message.
- 10, 20, 30, 45, 60 and 90-minute presets, or any whole-minute interval from 1 to 1,440.
- Yes, Not yet and dismiss controls, with a celebration or gentle reaction.
- A system tray / menu-bar menu to open Settings, test, pause, resume or quit.
- Optional startup at login, off until you enable it.
- Local preferences, without accounts, analytics, a backend or an AI service.

**Fixed in 1.9.2:** choosing a T-shirt color now covers the sleeves and sides during the reaction poses, including waving and celebrating.

## Downloads

- **Windows:** `HydroBuddy-Setup.exe`
- **Apple silicon Mac:** `HydroBuddy-1.9.2-mac-arm64.dmg`
- **Intel Mac:** `HydroBuddy-1.9.2-mac-x64.dmg`

Choose the installer under **Assets** for your computer. The automatic Source code archives are not installers. On a Mac, check **Apple menu → About This Mac** to find your chip, then open the DMG and drag HydroBuddy into Applications. macOS 13 or newer is required.

## Beta notes

- Windows has been tested locally. Mac support is experimental; physical Mac testing is still pending.
- Windows builds are unsigned. Mac beta builds are ad-hoc signed and not Apple-notarized, so security warnings or launch blocks are possible.
- Mac login startup needs verification with the final signed and notarized build.
- Primary-display placement, fullscreen applications, mixed display scaling and sleep/resume need broader testing.
- Face uploads, face replacement and automatic app updates are not included.

Existing preferences are preserved when updating. Quit HydroBuddy from its tray menu before installing the update.

Found a problem? [Open an issue](https://github.com/Vriddhi23/HydroBuddy/issues) with your operating system, Mac chip if relevant, and the steps to reproduce it. Screenshots and short recordings are welcome.
